# Elevator stand: results of the 16-18 Nov 2026 sessions, and the parameters to use

Builds EL35 to EL48. This follows `findings-2026-11-16.md` (EL27-EL35), which is still right about the mechanism, the control architecture and the characterization. Where the two disagree, this file wins. "Observed" means a robot log, a script's data or a test showed it. "Inferred" means we think so but did not prove it. Run ids are Forge runs on the Elevator Stand.

## 1. The parameters to use for this elevator

These are the code's defaults on `main` from commit f5a78ec (build EL48). **No jerk limit** (decided 18 Nov after the jerk sweep). Nothing has to be set by a script; the program starts with these.

**The one caution:** with no jerk limit the stand meets the voltage rule easily but sits **on** the 32 A current line, not under it. If the operational code needs real current margin, the measured options are (a) a jerk limit (7000 in/s^3 gave 28.4 A at 0.345 s, section 7) or (b) less acceleration (inferred, untested: current at the start of a move is mostly acceleration, so roughly 650 in/s^2 should give about 2 A back for about 0.01 s per move). With the jerk limit off by decision, (b) is the lever to try first.

The 0.5 s / 0.02 in goal from 15 Nov is met with about half the time to spare. These values are for **this stand** (3.96 lb carriage, 9.4 in travel, two Kraken X44, 15T/16T pinions). A real robot's elevator needs its own characterization with the same method.

## 2. Why the two motors seemed to differ (the load-sharing fault): solved

**The question (from 15-16 Nov):** with the left following the right at the same logged volts, the two motors drew very different currents, and which one was "odd" changed between routine starts.

**What we tested, in order, and what each showed (observed):**

| Test | Run, build | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Repeat-start, 12 solo starts | bd70db54, EL35 | "Apparent back-EMF" jumped between starts (1.2-3.4 V), never within one, on both motors |
| Tuner X self-test, both Talons | (by hand) | Firmware 26.3.0.0, every fault false incl. RotorFault1/2, not Pro-licensed. Rules out the known CTRE rotor-position bug (fixed in 26.2.0.0) |
| Trigger: config rewrite vs request switch | 60e9fff6, EL36 | Flips on both kinds of start: config writes are **not** the trigger |
| Open-loop VoltageOut ramps, 8 V/s, solo | eae3553c, EL38 | Volts vs speed **identical at every start** on both motors (about 2.1 V up / -1.45 V down at 20 in/s). The motors do not change state |
| kP 0 / 2 vs 8, solo | 6ace7cca, 5af07413, EL38-39 | kP 0 tripped the 0.9 in path stop; kP 2 still split. Talon's own readings inconsistent at kP 8 (torque current above stator current) |
| Follower gain test, both motors | bd0ca0b8, EL39 | At kP 8 the leader did all the work (14-16 A vs -1 to -2 A up) and they **fought going down** (-10 A vs +9 A). At kP 2 they mostly shared |
| Same test after PR #25 (1 kHz) | 938bf417, EL40 | **No more fighting on any of 12 starts.** Up 8.3-8.7 A / 10-12 A, down same sign. Talon supply current at 30 in/s fell about 1/4 going up, 1/2 going down. CAN use 32 % -> 57 % |

**The cause (observed in code and CTRE docs, confirmed by the fix):** a Phoenix 6 `Follower` updates its output only when the leader's `MotorVoltage` frame arrives. Our code sent that frame at 100 Hz while the leader's loop runs at 1 kHz, so at high gain the follower applied a stale 10 ms sample of a fast-changing output. The 100 Hz log recorded that same sample, so the logged volts matched while the real averages did not. The sampling phase differs from start to start, which made the "odd" motor change between starts.

**Lesson:** with a `Follower` on a high-gain loop, raise the leader's `MotorVoltage`/`DutyCycle` update frequency to 1 kHz. CAN cost was acceptable (57 %).

The left (16T) still carries a little more than the right while moving, as the gearing predicts (about 0.1 A per in/s more). That is physics, not a fault.

## 3. Instrumentation added (all still in the code)

- **PDH logging** (`PowerLog`, PR #26): voltage, total current, channels 10, 11, 22. PDH current never reads negative and is coarse (0.125 A per channel, 1-2 A total). Use it for true battery energy, the Talons for direction.
- **Loop breakdown and reading ages** (PR #27): the leader's P, D and FF parts, output, error, reference, all from one `refreshAll` per loop, with each reading's age in ms.
- **Current and volts on NetworkTables with robot-side peaks** (PR #30): `/Elevator/Status/PeakStatorAmps`, `PeakVoltsFractionOfBattery`, `/Power/Status/PeakPdhTotalAmps` and more, taken every 20 ms loop on the robot. Needed because the robot's WPILOGs were not reaching Forge's runs (section 9).
- **`/Elevator/Control/ResetPeaks`** (PR #33): restarts every peak without a new enable, so a sweep runs several sets in one enable after the auto-range.
- **Config write checks and Talon read-backs** (PR #32, PR #34): every write's StatusCode is checked; after each write the leader's Motion Magic and slot 0 are read back and published (`TalonJerkInPerSec3`, `TalonAccelInPerSec2`, `TalonkA`, `TalonkP`, `ConfigOk`, `ConfigStatus`, `ConfigWrites`). Writes wait 0.1 s, read-backs 0.25 s with 3 tries (50 ms gave `RxTimeout` on every read-back on the stand).
- **Profile timing** (PR #32): `LastProfileSeconds`, `LastProfileInches`, `ProfilesTimed`: the Talon's own Motion Magic profile duration per move, measured on the robot (20 ms resolution). This is the move-time number to trust.

## 4. Measurement lessons (observed)

- **The robot log records at 50 Hz** (one row per 20 ms loop), not 100 Hz. A 0.25 s move has about 12 samples.
- **Cruise "lag" between two signals is mostly timing.** Position and reference from different CAN frames are skewed by a few ms; at 30 in/s, 4 ms is 0.12 in; at 75 in/s, 7 ms is 0.5 in. Even the Talon's own `ClosedLoopError` disagreed with P/kP by about +/-0.15 in at 30 in/s. Use **holding error, overshoot and stop repeatability** for accuracy, not mid-move path error. The "worst path error" figures in this file (0.1-0.5 in) are mostly this skew.
- **At kP >= 6 the Talon's 100 Hz readings stop adding up** (applied volts and loop output differ by 0.4-0.9 V RMS, up to 2 V; the P part is often opposite in sign to the error). Inferred: the output swings faster than any 100 Hz signal shows. kP 4 avoids this.
- **Read peaks on the robot, not from script samples.** A script sampling at ~50 Hz saw 30.3 A where the robot's per-loop peak was 31.54 A.
- **Peak stator current varies about 2 A run to run** at the same settings (30.2-31.9 A at jerk 0). Compare sets inside one enable, and do not credit a difference under ~2 A to a setting.
- **Check what the Talon really holds.** The first jerk sweep (EL45) wrote 10000-40000 in/s^3, above CTRE's 0-9999 range for `MotionMagicJerk`; nothing told us. Clamp to the vendor's range and read the value back before trusting a sweep.

## 7. Jerk sweep (observed; jerk not adopted)

One enable, EL48 (f5a78ec), run 8f6d0aae, 90 / 700, kP 4, kA 0.0016, 3 full-travel starts (12 moves) per value. Every value was read back from the Talon before its set. Peaks reset per set.

| Jerk (in/s^3) | Move time (Talon profile, mean of 12) | Peak stator | Peak volts | Worst path error | Energy per start |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0 (off)** | **0.245 s** | 31.2 A | 56 % | 0.32 in | 94 J |
| 9999 (CTRE max) | 0.308 s | 38.4 A* | 54 % | 0.28 in | 83 J |
| 7000 | 0.345 s | 28.4 A | 38 % | 0.15 in | 64 J |
| 5000 | 0.380 s | 21.8 A | 40 % | 0.10 in | 46 J |
| 3500 | 0.440 s | 19.5 A | 34 % | 0.13 in | 37 J |

\*Two samples 20 ms apart on the right motor at the start of one down move; the left read -15.8 A at that instant. Otherwise the set never exceeded 30.8 A. Inferred, not proven: the two readings arrived at different times rather than the motors fighting; the robot's WPILOG would settle it.

- The added time matches the profile maths (about accel/jerk per move: 0.07 s at 9999, 0.10 s at 7000, 0.20 s at 3500).
- A gentler jerk costs time but cuts peak current, path error and energy: 7000 is 40 % slower than no limit, with 3 A less peak, half the path error and a third less energy.
- **Decision (director, 18 Nov): no jerk limit in the operational code.** Speed wins; the current margin is handled as in section 1.
- The first jerk sweep (EL45, 00:21, runs 8bbe44a5-df072cad) is **void**: four of its values were out of the Talon's range and nothing was read back.

- The FOC question was parked: the Talons are not Pro-licensed, and the fault turned out to be our update rate.
