# Elevator stand: what we know (15-16 Nov 2026)

The results of two evenings of work on the stand (builds EL27 to EL35), so that later work does not have to repeat them. "Observed" means a robot log, a script's data or a test showed it. "Inferred" means we think so but did not prove it. Run ids are Forge runs; each run's WPILOG is attached to it on the Forge site.

## Headline

- **The 0.5 s goal is met.** Run f72f9052 (EL34): full travel (8.94 in, 0.25 in inside each stop) in **0.27-0.29 s**, worst path error **0.09 in**, overshoot **0.006 in**, at 45 in/s, 400 in/s^2, jerk off, 6 V cap, 40 A limit, kP 8, kD 0.10. Peak 4.8 V; battery never below 10.7 V.
- **The code defaults are still the slow, safe ones** (10 in/s, 60 in/s^2, jerk off, 3 V, 20 A). The fast settings above are set by scripts over NetworkTables and put back at the end. Making them the default is the first next step (see `next-steps.md`).
- **One open fault:** the two motors do not share the load reliably. Each Talon seems to settle into a different electrical "state" at each routine start, which costs push (the motors brake each other), not accuracy. The mechanism has been ruled out. Details below.

## The mechanism (confirmed)

- Travel **9.375 +/- 0.25 in** by ruler. Auto-range measures **9.443-9.446 in**, repeatable within **0.003 in** across every run.
- Carriage 3.959 lb by CAD, without chains. Both Kraken X44 drive **one shared shaft**.
- Gearing: **right pinion 15T, left pinion 16T**, idlers, **46T** shaft gear (`CUS-001-068` is a part number, not 68T), 16T #25 sprockets: **4.0 in per shaft turn**. Right 0.7667, left 0.71875 motor turns per inch. Confirmed by EL27 (right turns 1.065x the left; 16/15 = 1.067) and by both encoders agreeing within 0.5% at cruise in every routine of run 2e332d79.
- **Mechanism is solid:** no slip, no flex measurable at cruise (encoder-derived speeds agree within 0.5%). The older 10-20% left/right velocity gaps came from the two velocity signals being sampled at different instants, not from the mechanism.
- Firmware: both Talons **26.3.0.0**, bootloader 0.3. Hardware revision **right (CAN 10) 4.2**, **left (CAN 11) 4.1**. Control is **non-FOC** (`MotionMagicVoltage`).
- Motor temperatures stayed at 30-32 C all evening.

## Control architecture: what works and what did not

| Build | Commit | Setup | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| EL27 | 73e2f69 | left `Follower`, one ratio for both | Right encoder said 10.08 in of travel: wrong ratio on the right (15T, not 16T) |
| EL28 | 46fca7e | per-motor ratios, a Motion Magic loop in **each** Talon | Scale fixed (9.444 in), but the two P loops fought through gear slack: opposed 35% of the hold |
| EL29 | 337a1b4 | left feedforward only, right corrects | Fight mostly gone (3%), but the right did all the holding |
| EL30 | 7014f58 | same, measured feedforward | Worse: left braked on descents, opposed 38% |
| **EL31** | 801eae2 | **left is a CTRE `Follower` (Opposed) of the right; one loop** | **Best: hold shared, 0% opposed in moves, path error 0.027 in.** Kept since. |

Lesson: with two motors on one shaft, run **one** closed loop and make the other motor a follower. Two loops (even with feedforward only on one) fight through backlash.

## Feedforward (measured, run cf3877f1, EL29)

Least squares on two 0.25 V/s ramps (R^2 0.96), kA from 1 V steps (rough), for the pair at the same volts:

| kS | kG | kV | kA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.122 V | 0.162 V | 0.0643 V/(in/s) | ~0.0010 V/(in/s^2) |

- The stand needs 14% more volts for gravity than the CAD weight predicts and acts as if it has ~1.6x the estimated moving mass (inferred: gears, shaft and sprockets).
- **kV sweep (run 77b36989, EL33): 0.0643, 0.070, 0.076, 0.082 all tracked within 0.03-0.04 in.** kV stays at 0.0643. An earlier "kV is too low" reading came from run-to-run noise of about +/-0.3 V in the extra voltage, not from kV.

## Speed, acceleration, jerk and current limit

Speed test 1 (run c62e2c0c, 6 V cap, standard moves): smooth at 25/35/45 in/s settings; peak 3.4 V, so the 6 V cap was never the limit. On a 7 in move the carriage never reaches 35 or 45 in/s; acceleration limits it.

Full travel, jerk compared (run 01a68463, EL34, 45 in/s, 200 in/s^2, **20 A** limit):

| Jerk | Crossing | Worst error |
|---|---|---|
| off | 0.40 s | 0.18 in (0.09 on repeat) |
| 4000 | 0.42-0.44 s | 0.05 in |
| 2000 | 0.48-0.49 s | 0.07 in |

A 0.40 in stumble (carriage slowed 27 -> 23 in/s for ~20 ms) was real, at peak acceleration with the 20 A limit.

Current limit raised to 40 A (run f72f9052, EL34, 8 crossings per row, worst of 8):

| Accel (in/s^2) | Jerk | Time | Worst error | Overshoot | Peak current L/R |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | off | 0.41 s | 0.04 in | 0.006 in | 15 / 25 A |
| 200 | 4000 | 0.41 s | 0.06 in | 0.015 in | 23 / 24 A |
| 300 | off | 0.33 s | 0.07 in | 0.009 in | 37 / 27 A |
| 300 | 6000 | 0.33 s | 0.26 in | 0.025 in | 44 / 35 A |
| **400** | **off** | **0.29 s** | **0.09 in** | **0.006 in** | 35 / 26 A |
| 400 | 8000 | 0.29 s | 0.15 in | 0.028 in | 44 / 25 A |

- **With 40 A, jerk off is best**; jerk only helped at 20 A because it kept the current under the limit. Inferred: jerk now just adds delay.
- At 400 in/s^2 the carriage reaches the 45 in/s cruise, so **top speed is now what limits crossing time**. More margin comes from raising cruise, not acceleration.
- Dal's rule (16 Nov): **optimise speed and accuracy, not sound.** Odd sounds are clues, not something to avoid.

## The load-sharing fault (open)

Observed:
- f72f9052: the left Talon drew **711 J** of the battery energy and the right **56 J** (767 J total). Winding-heat waste from the uneven split: **~224 J** (estimate, depends on an assumed 0.040 ohm winding resistance; range 18-29% of the battery energy, ~32% during moves). Heat is trivial (<0.1 C per crossing); the cost is **lost push**: on bad crossings the left pushed +41 A while the right braked at -22 A.
- At steady cruise both motors get the same volts and turn at the same speed, yet the right's apparent back-EMF constant wandered **0.008-0.026** between identical repeats; the left matched the data sheet (0.014-0.015 vs 0.0148).
- **Isolation test (run 2e332d79, EL35):** with one motor driving and the other coasting (zero current):
  - **Left alone, same move twice (A2 vs A4):** cruise 2.22 V / 17.5 A vs **1.46 V / 10.8 A**; going down -4.1 A vs **+4.3 A**; apparent back-EMF 0.011-0.013 vs **0.006-0.008**. Carriage speed identical.
  - Within a routine all crossings matched; the behaviour changed only **between** routines.
  - Both driving: the odd motor was the left in B1 (right leads), the right in B3 (right leads), mild in B2, none in B4 (left leads, best matched of the test).
- **Fault bit 512 = `StatorCurrLimit`** (now logged by name). It lit on whichever motor ran the loop in 6 of 8 routines, at **logged** 15-28 A with the limit at 40 A (the log samples every 20 ms and could miss spikes).

Inferred (not proven):
- **Not mechanical**: one motor alone needed fewer volts at the same speed in one routine than in another. Friction or backlash could raise current, not lower the volts a motor needs at a given speed.
- **Not one bad motor or hardware revision**: both motors do it.
- **Looks like a state each Talon settles into at a routine start and keeps until the next start.** The code reapplies configs and switches the control request at each start. Best guess: the Talon's rotor-angle (commutation) estimate in non-FOC mode. CTRE does not document this; it is a lead.
- The early-firing `StatorCurrLimit` flag may be related (the Talon may believe its current is higher than the logged value).

## Tooling facts learned

- **Forge's `robot_disable` fails while a script is running** (7 of 7 tries); it worked once after a script had ended. Rule since 16 Nov: scripts end **parked, motors off**, log **TEST DONE ... PLEASE DISABLE NOW**, and wait for the person's disable; faults rely on `/Forge/Abort` (zero output in robot code), never on Forge's disable.
- **Score from the robot's WPILOG, not the script.** The script sees ~10 readings a second over NetworkTables, only when values change; it missed peaks (0.065 vs real 0.105 in) and could not time 0.3 s moves. The robot log arrives on the run a few minutes after the disable.
- One robot WPILOG covers everything since the program started (deploy), so several runs can share one file (EL33's three sessions are all in `FRC_20261116_012205`).
- **Path error must come from the Talon's own closed-loop error** (`ProfileErrorInches`, since EL33). Subtracting separately read position and reference was ~5 ms skewed and read up to 0.37 in too high at 25 in/s.
- **Current readings sit 0.2-0.6 A off zero when idle**: count "motors opposed" only when both exceed 1 A in opposite directions.
- A script's `limits` on a topic fail if the topic has no value yet: wait for `PositionInches` to appear after a deploy first.
- Auto-range's top creep stalls each motor at ~16 A for ~0.85 s at 0.5 V; that looks like a spike in the log but is normal.
- Tuning writes to the Talons happen only while disabled or **parked**, so a sweep runs in one enable (park, write, run, park).
